Patient Clinical & Operational Lifecycle
How a patient flows through the U-Konek system from daily queueing to at-home medication compliance.
1
Queue & Arrival
Patient generates a ticket via Mobile App or registers at the clinic as a walk-in. Ticket is visible on the Waiting Room TV display.
2
Nurse Triage
Nurse records blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, height, weight, and automated BMI calculation in vital_signs.
3
Doctor Consultation
Doctor reviews triage vitals, inputs symptoms, HPI, PMH, differential diagnosis, and orders diagnostics into consultations.
4
Electronic Rx (e-Prescription)
Doctor prescribes medication items with dosage, frequency, and duration. A unique RX-XXXXXX code is generated.
5
Pharmacy Dispensing
Pharmacist pulls up the prescription code, verifies stock, and records partial or full dispensing with inventory deduction.
6
At-Home Intake Alarms
Citizen mobile app syncs active prescriptions, sets intake alarm reminders, and logs daily taken doses in medicine_intake_logs.
